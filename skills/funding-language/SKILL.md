---
name: Funding language
description: Read funding announcements and distinguish pre-seed from later rounds.
jobs: [hunt.look, scout.hackernews, investigate.company, company.traction]
words: [funding, raised, pre-seed, seed, Series A]
priority: 80
---
Use the page's dated wording and name the announced instrument or round. A grant, accelerator cheque, angel investment or explicitly named pre-seed is not the same as a seed round. Treat “pre-seed” as pre-seed only when the source uses that label or clearly says it is the first institutional round. A missing announcement does not establish that no money was raised.
