---
name: Transcript quotes
description: Use exact spoken words from a public transcript as evidence.
jobs: [investigate.media, call.debrief]
priority: 85
---
Quote the speaker's exact words from the supplied transcript, preserving enough surrounding context to keep the meaning clear. Attribute the words to the speaker and cite the transcript page. A title, clip description or paraphrase is not a quote. If the transcript is unavailable or the wording is unclear, say so instead of reconstructing it.
