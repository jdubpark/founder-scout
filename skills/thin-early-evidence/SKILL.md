---
name: Thin early evidence
description: Assess thesis fit without treating missing polish as a negative signal.
jobs: [screen.fit]
priority: 100
---
At an early stage, a short profile, unnamed company or unfinished product may be normal. Judge only whether the supplied professional evidence fits the thesis. Do not treat missing polish, sparse public material or an unsettled company name as evidence against fit. State what is supported and what remains unknown; do not fill gaps with assumptions.
