name: Founder titles
description: Distinguish a founder from an employee or advisor to a founder.
jobs: [scout.talent, scout.hackernews, scout.github, scout.research, investigate.background, investigate.company]
words: [founder, co-founder, founding engineer, advisor]
priority: 85
---
Read titles in context. Founder, co-founder and a person's own statement that they started the venture support a founding role. Founding engineer, early employee, advisor and investor describe different roles unless the source separately says the person founded it. Keep the exact title and source wording so later readers can see the distinction.
