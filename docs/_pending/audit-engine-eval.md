## Contracts and keepers

- `engine/index.ts`, `engine/pipeline.ts`, and `engine/final-stages.ts` — stage order, retry and run/task spend ceilings, bounded concurrency, durable failure/recovery, pool routing, and runs never dispatch/dial outreach or overwrite a person's call decision. Keepers: `engine/index.test.ts > "stops starting tasks at the run spend cap after recording the task that crossed it"`, `engine/index.test.ts > "treats the task and run spend caps as ceilings across retries"`, `engine/index.test.ts > "runs independent model calls concurrently within the configured limit and stays deterministic"`, `engine/index.test.ts > "drafts simulated outreach only for the latest yes call and never dispatches it"`, `engine/index.test.ts > "does not dispatch a live approved call action"`, `engine/index.test.ts > "keeps an approved simulated call untouched during a run"`, and `engine/index.test.ts > "does not overwrite a No recorded while the analyst task is in flight"`.
- `engine/index.ts` — quote checking precedes fact-checking; every fact-check call has a planted false claim; accepting it discards that call's verdicts, and unsupported evidence cannot score. Keepers: `engine/index.test.ts > "checks live web quotes before fact-checking and respects both quote-check switches"`, `engine/index.test.ts > "plants one disposable control and discards the call if the checker supports it"`, `engine/index.test.ts > "does not score a founder whose evidence was never fact-checked"`, and `engine/index.test.ts > "treats an answer that cites no verified evidence as unsettled, so it cannot surface a founder"`.
- `engine/index.ts` — recording allowance is checked before transcription; an existing transcript is not repeated; personal attributes are redacted before storage/model use; transcript quotes retain time-coded support. Keepers: `engine/index.test.ts > "refuses a recording that cannot fit the run allowance before calling the transcriber"`, `engine/index.test.ts > "does not transcribe a queued recording that already has a transcript"`, `engine/index.test.ts > "redacts transcript text and segments before storage and media.read input, and records transcription cost"`, and `engine/index.test.ts > "transcribes queued simulated recordings and keeps only transcript quotes"`.
- `engine/index.ts` — model pool routing honors a person's pin, deterministic choice, and a member failing out; simulated fields stay out of prompts. Keepers: `engine/index.test.ts > "routes simulated investigation tasks to stable pool members unless a person pins the model"`, `engine/index.test.ts > "leaves out a pool member that is down, so a dead model cannot stall the hunt"`, and `engine/index.test.ts > "keeps company facts and raw call transcripts out of the founder analyst input"`.
- `engine/hunt.ts` — hunt spend share is reserved across concurrent calls; a navigator cannot undo a person's pin, pause or close; runner names, quotes and addresses must be on-page; planted pages, too-late rounds, paused hosts, hunt-only stage cutoff, outside-agent seat, and navigator `done` are applied at the engine boundary. - `engine/company-stages.ts` — founder and company decisions jointly determine stored deal; a person-recorded No/Yes is preserved; company evidence/citations and exact identity grouping remain distinct from founder evidence; only one deep second look is used.
