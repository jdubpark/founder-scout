# Dashboard (apps/web)

Scope: the whole operator dashboard, every route. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: a venture partner who is not an engineer opens this each morning to see who the swarm surfaced, read why, decide who to meet, approve or reject drafted outreach, and tell the agents what to change. A technical operator uses the same screens to watch cost, failures and the improvement loop.

Constraints: every score traces to its sources on the same screen; simulated, estimated and unverified values are always labelled as such; state is never carried by colour alone; standard web controls only.

Unresolved: fund name and thesis; whether a dark theme is wanted; authentication for a hosted deployment.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The dashboard is the fund's own offering document about people: each morning's surfaced founders are announced in ranked brackets, and every figure carries its source. It refuses the category default of a grey admin grid with one accent colour and a row of big-number tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Securities print. A deep engraving-green rail, pale safety-paper ground, ink that is green-black rather than grey. Seal gold is reserved for "surfaced"; legend red is reserved for cautions (simulated, unverified, flagged) and never used for decoration or primary actions; bistre brown carries the watchlist and secondary series. Caslon in capitals for names and headings, a Franklin gothic for everything operated. Square corners, hairline rules, dense tabular figures. States are printed marks with a label: filled, hollow, struck, paused.

STORY: The partner sees who is worth a meeting today and why, trusts it because the proof sits beside each claim, acts on the few things that need a person, and can see exactly what each agent was told after steering it.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 216px green rail on the left holds the swarm switcher, eight destinations with a count on Approvals, and the run status at the foot. To its right, a page header with the swarm name, thesis line and a "Run now" button. The main column opens with the surfaced list set in three ranked brackets (lead name largest, then a row of two to four, then running columns), score figures hanging right, tier rules between brackets. The right column, 320px, is "Needs you": approvals waiting with the age of the oldest, founders awaiting a reaction, and alerts. Below the fold: the funnel, spend against budget, the seven agent classes with their state marks.

SIGNATURE: Rank is carried typographically, the way a syndicate announcement orders its names: position and size are the ranking, and the rules between brackets are the decision thresholds. Raised by declined challengers: one control re-ranks the whole list live while it is dragged, with a count readout, before anything is saved (type specimen); each claim sits beside its proof, never on another tab (claim and proof pairing); each panel header says what needs a person next, with a count and an age (airport wayfinding); one colour per meaning, used nowhere else (orienteering map); state shown as a printed mark plus a word (reference setting).

FORM: Tombstone and red-herring prospectus typography, candidate 6 of 7 on the ordered list, assigned by the roll. seed:b04706613fab1e4d. Code-led; no comp.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
