# Dashboard (apps/web)

Scope: the whole operator dashboard, every route. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: a venture partner who is not an engineer opens this each morning to see who the swarm surfaced, say yes or no with a reason, approve drafted outreach, and tell the agents what to change. A technical operator uses the same screens to watch cost, failures and the improvement loop.

Constraints: every score traces to its sources on the same screen; simulated data is always marked; state is never carried by colour alone; standard web controls only; light theme only; desktop only.

Unresolved: fund name and thesis; authentication for a hosted deployment.

## Direction contract

The owner pinned the direction on 2026-11-12, replacing the rolled one: light mode, very simple, professional, modelled on a well-known team chat app, with only the text that is necessary (no subtitles, captions, footnotes or descriptive text) and simple diagrams and icons in place of words.

THESIS: The dashboard should feel like the chat tool the partner already uses all day: swarms are listed like channels, founders are rows you act on, and steering is a note you send. It refuses the dense admin console and the explanatory paragraph.

OWN-WORLD: Deep purple sidebar, white work area, one typeface (Lato) in three weights, rounded lightly bordered regions, pill tags, red count badges. Green is the action and "yes"; blue is selection and focus; yellow marks "surfaced"; red is a count that needs you or a failure.

STORY: The partner sees what needs them as counts in the sidebar, works down a list saying yes or no, and can see and steer the swarm as one simple line of teams.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A 240px purple sidebar: product name, Home, Founders and Approvals with red counts, then the swarms listed like channels, then the manage pages, with the run status at the foot. To its right a white page: a bold title and the page's one action, then the surfaced founders as rows with avatar, name, score, and Yes / No.

SIGNATURE: The swarm drawn as one line of seven teams with a count on each, which opens a team's jobs when clicked, and a Yes / No on every founder row.

FORM: Team-chat product layout, pinned by the owner. seed: none (direction pinned by the user after the roll seed:b04706613fab1e4d). Code-led; no comp.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
